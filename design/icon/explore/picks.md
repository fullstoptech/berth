# Icon round 1 picks (black only, 16 marks per route)

Source: `design/icon/berth-icon.figcraft`, page "Explore". Marks are `explore/<route>/NN.svg`; sheets are `explore/<route>/contact-sheet.png`; the 32 px strips are `explore/<route>/strip-32.png` (4x). Judged on the rubric: silhouette distinct at 32 px, one idea, not a taken shape.

## MOORING
- **11** (bollard wrapped in rope): the strongest silhouette of the route; the two diagonal cuts are the one feature and survive at 32 px. Risk: it shows the mooring but no container.
- **10** (block seated in a bay cut into a slab): one block, one slot, reads as "rests here" at 32 px. Risk: close to a memory-card slot.
- **15** (arch berth with a block inside): calm, simple, holds at 32 px. Risk: reads as a tunnel or door.
- Weak: 03 (anvil), 05 (hat), 14 (V sling), 16 (too many parts).

## ORIGIN
- **12** (folder-tab half fused to container ribs): says "folder and container are one thing" in a single silhouette, the stepped top edge is the only feature, and it holds at 32 px. Not the Finder shape.
- **06** (slashed block, tab on the left half): the slash is a path separator; strong at 32 px. Risk: "folder with a slash" can read as deleted.
- **07** (chamfered ribbed tab body): corrugation plus a tab, distinct cut corners. Risk: reads as an SD card.
- Weak: 05 (pocket) now reads as a USB stick, 08 is a taken cube, 13 and 16 are too close to a folder.

## NAME
- **12** (lowercase b, bowl is a slip holding one block): letter and idea in one feature; the square in the bowl survives at 32 px. Not a taken shape.
- **08** (outline B, block docked in the lower bowl): same idea in a capital B; thinner, so it needs a heavier stroke before it is safe at 32 px.
- **01** (B of two stacked D blocks): the most robust at 32 px; the stack of containers is the idea. Risk: generic bold B.
- Weak: 03 reads as sunglasses, 04 and 11 are muddy, 16 clots at 32 px.

## Put forward overall: NAME 12
The only mark where the berth (the bowl) visibly holds a container, and it is also a letter. One feature, flat, simple geometry, and the counter stays open at 32 px. Runners-up for a second direction: MOORING 10 (no letter, pure place) and ORIGIN 12 (carries the folder idea).
